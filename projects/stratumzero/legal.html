Legal notes

Renders and the 3D model come from the KiCad 10 design. Component models are from the KiCad libraries (CC BY-SA 4.0 with the KiCad library exception), the manufacturer (the USB-C jack) or were drawn for this project from datasheets; the OCXO, OLED and several connectors are simplified envelopes. The viewer uses three.js (MIT). The compute module design is open at OrbitalCommons/affogato.
