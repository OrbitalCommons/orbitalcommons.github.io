OrbitalCommons

High-quality, open-source libraries and tools for satellite imaging, satellite navigation, and space exploration. Free for astronomers and commercial enterprise alike.

The foundational tools for working with space data should be fast, reliable, and accessible to everyone. We use the right language for each job: Rust where performance and safety matter most, Python where the ecosystem and ergonomics win.

What we are building toward

  • Satellite imaging. Capturing, storing and processing astronomical image data on open standards like FITS.
  • Satellite navigation. Blind astrometry and star field identification for autonomous celestial navigation.
  • Space exploration. Simulation, planning and analysis libraries that lower the barrier to working with space data.

Whether you are running a university observatory, building a CubeSat, or pointing a telescope in your back garden, these tools are for you. Contributions and ideas welcome.
