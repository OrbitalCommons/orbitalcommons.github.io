The stack

From photons to answers.

Measured, not promised

Lost in space? Found in about a second.

zodiacal is benchmarked on 1,000 simulated fields with known pointing (9568×6380 px, 0.13″/px, about 20′×13′), with no hint of where the camera was aimed. The best configuration solves 985 of them.

Principles

Built like it has to fly.

Correct first

Results are compared against reference implementations like Skyfield and SPICE, benchmarked on fields with known answers, and regression-tested on curated real data.

Fast and portable

Pure Rust with no system libraries to install. The same code builds for servers, single-board computers at the telescope, and the browser via WebAssembly.

Open to everyone

Permissive MIT, Apache-2.0 and BSD licences. Use it in a university observatory, a CubeSat, a commercial constellation or a telescope in your back garden.
